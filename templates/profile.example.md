# Profile

Copy to `profile.md` (gitignored) and fill it in. Every agent in the pipeline
reads this first. It is how they know who you are **now**, so they can tell an
old position from a current one. Short is fine. Wrong is worse than short.

## Who I am, briefly

<3 to 5 sentences: what you do, what you care about, what you're working on>

## Current positions

What you believe or have decided now, with dates where you know them. These
are the "now" side of every delta the agents will look for.

- **<topic>** (since <YYYY-MM>): <position>
- **<topic>** (since <YYYY-MM>): <position>

## Known changes

Positions you know changed. Mirror these in `position_changes` in your config.

- <YYYY-MM-DD> <topic>: was <old>, now <new>

## Don't

Framing the agents should never use about you, even if old conversations are
full of it. Example: "I dropped the startup-founder framing; don't describe my
plans in growth or scale language."

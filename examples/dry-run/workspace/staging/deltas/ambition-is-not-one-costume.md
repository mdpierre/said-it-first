# Ambition is not one particular costume

## Current position (2025-07)

Ambition does not have to take one specific, recognisable form, and choosing a
different form is not settling. By mid-2025 he was sorting his own goals into
the ones he actually cares about and the ones he thinks he should want, and
noticing that the second kind is what he talks about with other people.

> "My fear was that I'd be settling, but I think I was confusing ambition with a specific costume of ambition."
> [[2024-03-02-rethinking-the-founder-plan]] (about 2024-03-10)

> "Get promoted, buy a nicer car, have a five year plan, and I notice the second list is the one I talk about at dinner"
> [[2025-07-01-two-lists]]

The list he says he actually cares about, same turn: "Travel more, write every
week, build one thing that is mine" (quoted from his turn; checked by hand).

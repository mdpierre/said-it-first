# Letting myself want things out loud

## Current position (2025-01)

He does want things, and wants to say so instead of deciding in advance that
he doesn't care. What he wants is to make things that are his and have people
see them.

> "And the thing is I actually do want stuff, I want to make things that are mine and I want people to see them"
> [[2025-01-15-walk-home-thoughts]] (dictated voice memo)

Six months later the same want shows up on the list of goals he says he
actually cares about: "build one thing that is mine" ([[2025-07-01-two-lists]],
short quote, checked by hand).

## How this changed

### before-2025-01 -> 2025-01
**Was:** Deciding ahead of time that he doesn't care, so that not getting
something can't hurt; saying it doesn't matter often enough to half believe it.
He describes this as a habit held since childhood.

> "I basically never let myself want things out loud, like I just kind of decide ahead of time that I don't care so that it can't hurt."
> "I'm so used to saying it doesn't matter that I believe it for a second."
> [[2025-01-15-walk-home-thoughts]] (2025-01-15, dictated voice memo)

**Now:** He names what he wants (quote above) and says he wants to stop
pre-deciding. That closing line follows an assistant synthesis that is cut off
in the export, so it cannot be shown whether "pre-deciding" was his word or
the assistant's:
> **unverifiable**: "That's right. I want to stop pre-deciding I don't care."
> [[2025-01-15-walk-home-thoughts]]

**Because:** He noticed the pattern on a walk home. He traces it to a role he
took on at home as a kid:
> "I think I've been doing that since I was a kid, you know, because it was easier to be the easy one in the house."
> [[2025-01-15-walk-home-thoughts]]

and says he is "not mad about it", he just notices it now.

The memo appears three times in the export (a dictation artifact); it is
quoted once here. The Was side is his own description at the moment of
noticing. No earlier conversation shows the old habit in action.

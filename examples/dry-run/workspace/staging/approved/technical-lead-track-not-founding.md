# Technical lead track, not founding a company

## Current position (2024-03)

He wants the technical lead track: not management, and not founding a
company. What he likes is working out how a thing works and making it run, and
being trusted with hard problems. None of that needs him to own the business,
and owning it would mostly mean admin.

> "I told my manager I want the technical lead track, not the management track, and it felt right saying it out loud."
> [[2024-03-02-rethinking-the-founder-plan]] (last turn of a conversation started 2024-03-02; about 2024-03-10 by `span_hours`)

> "The line I keep coming back to is that I would rather be trusted with hard problems than be the one who owns them."
> [[2025-07-15-said-it-first]] (his turn 1, before the assistant quoted it back; he says he first wrote it on a napkin about a month earlier)

His reason, in the next turn of the same conversation: ownership is mostly
admin to him ("ownership is mostly admin to me", short quote, checked by hand).
